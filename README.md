# prismatic7

I'm Chris — a sound designer and researcher working in theatre and performance, teaching at University of the Arts Sharjah in the UAE. I build tools because the ones I need don't exist, and I believe that if you can't make a tool do what you want it to do, it's not your tool.

Using my tools the way I want to is art. My zsh setup is art. The months I've spent refining my Hermes agent — the one that helps me write, rehearse, and think — is art. The repos here are the parts of that practice I can share.

## The things that are me

**callsheet** — a calm, minimal desktop day-board for macOS. Colour-coded cards for research, making, teaching, body, admin. It holds, it never pings. I built it because I needed a thing no one else was doing. A friend said: *"As an ADHD'er constantly on the hunt for gentle & nice planning/prioritising/executing dashboards…this looks incredible Wenno!!"* I didn't make it for her. But it resonated. That's the whole point.

**loreweaver** — a local-first TTRPG tool, currently running a FATE of Cthulhu campaign. A hobby, and it's mine.

**Hermes** — I live in Hermes Agent. This profile, my federation across machines, the plugins I maintain for it — that's where the real work happens. Most of it is private, because it's a rehearsal room, not a show. But the practice is the point.

## Coming

**Enodios** — an Obsidian plugin, named for Hermes as protector of travellers and god of crossroads. It's the direction I'm heading. Not ready to talk about yet.

**A Zotero plugin** — for the way I actually do research. Name TBD.

## What I maintain

A set of plugins for the BB agentic IDE — unslop, noema, headroom, noisegate, rtk, progressive-skill, security-guidance. The platform is BB's; I maintain these for the ecosystem. Where the patterns are mine, they're mine; where they build on the work of others, that work is credited. These are transitional — the work I'm proudest of is the work that's mine.

## Where I stand

A space I set up is queer friendly. It yields space to voices that aren't mine. It stands *with*, not *for*.

My code is here for who *you* are, so long as you give the same affordance to others.

Also: no fucking Nazis.
